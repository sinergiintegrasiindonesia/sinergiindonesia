About Us

Bringing people, processes, and technology together.

PT Sinergi Integrasi Indonesia is an Indonesian company specialising in IT consulting, equipment provisioning, and technical support — focused on helping organisations build and maintain dependable technology environments.

Our name says it all: Sinergi (synergy) and Integrasi (integration). We help organisations bring people, processes, and technology together so each part strengthens the other.

We are a young company, and every member of our team brings real-world experience from the IT and consulting industry. We combine that expertise with the agility, attentiveness, and personal commitment that only a fresh, focused partner can offer.

Products

Privileged access, under control

We supply, deploy, and support JumpServer — the open-source Privileged Access Management platform — on hardware sized for your environment.

JumpServer secures and records every privileged connection into your infrastructure. Instead of engineers holding standing root credentials, access is brokered through one audited gateway — with multi-factor authentication in front and a full session recording behind.

It is fully open source under GPL-3.0, so there is no black box and no vendor lock-in, and a standard deployment goes from zero to production in about thirty minutes using Docker Compose.

Authentication

LDAP / AD sync, SSO via OIDC, OAuth2, SAML 2.0 and CAS, TOTP and WebAuthn MFA, IP and time-window restrictions.

Authorization

Role-based access control and just-in-time permissions, so each engineer reaches only the assets their role allows.

Account Management

Automated account discovery, credential collection, and scheduled password rotation across your estate.

Audit

Full session recording, command filtering and blocking, and real-time alerting for compliance evidence.
